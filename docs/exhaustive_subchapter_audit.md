This document exists to separate two different questions that were getting mixed together:

- Was every sub-chapter reviewed? Yes.

- continuity with neighboring pages
- tone and narrative quality
- quiz format consistency
- reference integrity
- formula and code-example risk

`Edits applied` means at least one side of the EN/KO pair was updated during this overhaul. In a few cases that update was a Korean-only quiz-format normalization rather than a substantive rewrite. `No additional edits required` means the page was reviewed but did not need further changes after the full pass.
