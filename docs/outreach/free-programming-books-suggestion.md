Suggested title: **Book suggestion: Foundation Model Engineering (English and Korean)**

The November 18 draft follows. The linked issue contains the submitted text.

I maintain Foundation Model Engineering and would like to suggest its English and Korean editions for the free books lists.

- Author: Seongeun So
- English: https://sungeuns.github.io/foundation-model-engineering/
- Korean: https://sungeuns.github.io/foundation-model-engineering/ko/
- Source: https://github.com/sungeuns/foundation-model-engineering
- Format/access: HTML; the full book is free to read without an account or email.
- Status: actively revised; the “in process” notation is appropriate.

The book covers foundation-model architectures, training, inference, retrieval, agents, and evaluation. Chapters combine mathematical explanations, worked examples, code, quizzes, and optional interactive demonstrations. The Korean edition may be useful alongside the existing Korean machine-learning books.

The project is independent and developed with AI assistance, disclosed on its homepage. This suggestion was also prepared with AI assistance. I checked the two proposed lists and searched existing issues and pull requests for the repository URL; I did not find an existing listing.
